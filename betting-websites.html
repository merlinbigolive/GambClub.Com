How to compare betting websites

Betting websites can differ significantly in licensing, markets, payment options, withdrawal rules and account features. Use this checklist to compare platforms using information you can verify.

Operator and licence

Identify the legal operator behind the website and check the licence information against the relevant regulator where applicable.

Markets and rules

Review the sports and markets offered and read settlement, void, cancellation and dispute rules before using a platform.

Deposits and withdrawals

Compare payment methods, limits, verification requirements and stated processing times.

Safer gambling

Look for deposit limits, time-outs, self-exclusion and other responsible-gambling controls.
