Online casinos: what to check before choosing a platform

An online casino can offer a wide selection of games and payment methods, but the important details vary from one operator to another. GambClub's guide explains the practical checks to make before creating an account.

Check licensing

Look for a clearly identified operator and the regulator or licensing information it publishes. A licence claim should be checked against the regulator's own records where possible.

Review banking terms

Compare supported deposit and withdrawal methods, minimums, fees, processing expectations and any verification requirements.

Read bonus terms

Promotional offers can include wagering requirements, maximum winnings, eligible games, expiry dates and other conditions. Read the full terms before claiming an offer.

Understand account rules

Check age requirements, identity verification, geographic restrictions, account limits and the operator's responsible-gambling tools.
